# Product Overview — Deal Sidekick

## What It Is
Deal Sidekick is an all-in-one real estate investment platform. It is NOT just a property analysis tool. That was v1. The app now covers the entire investor workflow from lead discovery to deal closing.

## Core Features (all live, all available to users)

### 1. Lead Discovery & Foreclosure Tracking
- Live foreclosure notice feed for Georgia counties (auto-scraped daily)
- Interactive map with filterable pins (county, status, date range)
- Auction calendar with countdown timers
- Saved searches with email alerts when new leads match criteria

### 2. Property Analysis Engine
- AI-powered investment analysis for any US property address
- Instant rent estimates, ARV, repair costs, ROI calculations
- Multiple investment strategies compared (buy & hold, flip, BRRRR, wholesale, Airbnb)
- Sold comps, market trends, neighborhood data
- Shareable PDF reports

### 3. AI Investment Copilot (Leo)
- Conversational AI assistant that understands your portfolio
- Ask questions about any property, market, or strategy
- Context-aware: knows your saved analyses, goals, and preferences
- Can run analyses, look up data, and explain complex concepts

### 4. CRM (Contact Relationship Manager)
- Track sellers, buyers, agents, and other contacts
- Log interactions (calls, emails, meetings)
- AI-powered interaction parsing (paste a note → structured data)
- Organization management with team roles
- Follow-up reminders and pipeline tracking

### 5. Portfolio Dashboard
- All saved property analyses in one view
- Portfolio-level metrics (total equity, cash flow, ROI)
- Property comparison tool (side-by-side)
- Custom property photos and notes

### 6. Lead Lists & Deal Management
- Create custom lead lists from foreclosure data
- Track deal status through your pipeline
- Property preference system (favorite, hidden, not interested)
- Bulk actions on leads
- Export lists as CSV with analytics
- Share lists as branded images

### 7. Credit System & Subscriptions
- Free tier: 1 analysis + limited leads
- Starter: 10 analyses/month + full lead access
- Pro: Unlimited analyses + CRM + copilot + priority support
- Elite: Everything + lead funnels + AI site builder + credit sharing
- Credit sharing: Elite members can share credits with investors (percentage allocations, one-time grants, clawback, activity monitoring)
- Credits for pay-as-you-go beyond plan limits

### 8. Lead Funnels (Elite)
- Custom landing pages for motivated sellers
- Embeddable forms with auto-CRM integration
- Conversion tracking and analytics

### 9. AI Site Builder (Elite)
- Conversational AI generates professional real estate websites
- Automatic integrations (CRM, property analysis, booking)
- Hosted on custom subdomains

### 10. Notifications & Alerts
- In-app notification center
- Email alerts for saved searches, new leads, auction dates
- Push notifications (mobile app)

### 11. Referral Program
- Users earn credits for referring new investors
- Tracked via unique referral codes

## Target Audience
Real estate investors — specifically:
- Wholesalers looking for distressed properties
- Fix-and-flip investors analyzing deals
- Buy-and-hold landlords evaluating rental properties
- New investors learning the market

## DealSidekick vs Fortune Leo
Same app, different brand. DealSidekick is the investor-focused white-label that hides the financial consulting content and shows only investment tools. Fortune Leo is the parent brand with broader financial consulting services.

## Key Differentiators (for marketing copy)
- Only platform that combines live foreclosure data + instant AI analysis + CRM in one tool
- Covers Georgia foreclosure market with daily-updated county data
- AI copilot that actually understands real estate investment math
- Mobile app with push notifications for time-sensitive auction leads

## When Writing Store Listings, Descriptions, or Marketing
ALWAYS reference the full feature set above. The app is a PLATFORM, not a single-feature tool. Lead with the breadth: "discover deals → analyze properties → manage contacts → close deals" — the full investor workflow.
